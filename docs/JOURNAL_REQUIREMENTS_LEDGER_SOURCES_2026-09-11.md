# Journal Requirements — Official Source Refresh, 2026-09-11

This note preserves the current official public-source refresh used to close construction of the IJIO Journal Requirements Ledger. Journal-specific unresolved rules remain governed by the evidence hierarchy in `docs/JOURNAL_REQUIREMENTS_LEDGER.md`.

## Official sources rechecked

- IJIO official Elsevier page: `https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/international-journal-of-industrial-organization/0167-7187`
  - verifies journal identity, ISSN `0167-7187`, EARIE relationship, and scope covering theoretical and empirical industrial organization, strategic behavior, technological change and regulation.
- IJIO Guide for Authors: `https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-journal-of-industrial-organization/publish/guide-for-authors`
  - controlling journal-specific source identified; the body was not reliably retrievable through the public automated path used in this audit, so inaccessible journal-specific rules are not promoted to PASS.
- Elsevier LaTeX instructions: `https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/policies-and-guidelines/latex-instructions`
  - states that most journals accept a PDF initially; journal-specific exceptions control; source bundles must include the manuscript source files and dependencies when requested.
- Elsevier manuscript-template support, updated 2026-08-28: `https://service.elsevier.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/5955/supporthub/publishing/~/is-there-a-template-available-for-my-manuscript-file%3F/`
  - reiterates that most journals accept initial PDF but directs authors to the journal Guide for Authors for exceptions/template requirements.
- Elsevier Highlights: `https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/tools-and-resources/highlights`
  - 3–5 bullets; each no more than 85 characters including spaces; not part of editorial consideration and generally not required until final-files stage, subject to journal-specific override.
- Elsevier generative-AI policies for journals: `https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals`
  - current policy requires disclosure of substantive AI-assisted manuscript preparation immediately before the references; AI use in the research process should be described in the research/methods documentation; human authors retain responsibility.
- Elsevier research-data guidelines: `https://www-prod.elsevier.com/researcher/author/tools-and-resources/research-data/data-guidelines`
  - journals use different policy options; authors must consult the chosen journal's Guide for Authors. IJIO's exact option is therefore left UNVERIFIED.
- Elsevier data statement: `https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/tools-and-resources/research-data/data-statement`
  - data-statement behavior is integrated for many journals, but exact target-journal obligations remain Guide/portal specific.
- Elsevier artwork overview/checklist: `https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/author/artwork-and-media-instructions/artwork-overview` and `https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/author/artwork-and-media-instructions/artwork-formats-checklist`
  - EPS/PDF/TIFF/JPEG accepted/recommended; PDF is acceptable for vector artwork.
- Elsevier pricing: `https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/pricing`
  - distinguishes subscription and open-access publication models; hybrid subscription publication can be used without an author APC. This does not certify absence of a submission-stage IJIO-specific charge.
- Elsevier double-anonymized review guidance: `https://www.elsevier.com/en-au/reviewer/what-is-peer-review/guidelines`
  - if a journal uses double-anonymized review, separate title page and anonymized manuscript requirements apply. IJIO applicability remains UNVERIFIED until the journal-specific Guide or authenticated portal confirms its review model.
